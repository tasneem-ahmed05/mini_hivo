Therefore the observed blocker was **failure to progress after reported sufficiency**, not sustained refusal to authorize mutations.

Authorization is an internal decision, not independent proof that the model possessed every needed fact or understood the task. Repeated passes do not establish that context selection is semantically correct. The trace does not isolate whether model behavior, duplicated instructions, tool-result presentation, retained conversation state or controller/tool availability caused repetition.

Separate existing `progress_constrained` branches restrict availability after success and apply progress stopping logic, with helper state in [worker_progress.py](../../hivo/worker_progress.py). That is a plausible available route for repetition. It is **not proof** that selecting another policy would make this task succeed. No alternate policy experiment or Worker correction was performed here.
